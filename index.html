How to Play

📦 Push wooden crates onto the glowing golden target spots.
🚫 You can only push one box at a time. Boxes cannot be pulled.
🧱 Walls block both you and the boxes. Beware of corners!
⌨️ Controls: WASD or Arrows to move, U / Z to Undo, R to Restart.
📱 Mobile: Tap the virtual D-Pad buttons or swipe on the game board.
